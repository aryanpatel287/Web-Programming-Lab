Who We Are

About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering at GEC Dahod is committed to providing quality technical education in the field of computing and information technology. We prepare students for careers in software development, data science, networking, and emerging technologies.

Our curriculum is regularly updated to align with industry standards and Gujarat Technological University (GTU) guidelines, ensuring graduates are career-ready from day one.

What We Offer

Academic Programs

Undergraduate and postgraduate programmes designed for tomorrow's engineers.

B.E. Computer Engineering

4-year undergraduate programme covering core CS fundamentals, web development, databases, and more.

  • Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
  • Intake: 60 Students
  • Affiliated: GTU
M.E. Computer Engineering

2-year postgraduate programme with specialisations in advanced computing and research areas.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Research-oriented
  • Affiliated: GTU
