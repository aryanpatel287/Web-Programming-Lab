Government Engineering College Dahod

Approved by AICTE and affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU), GEC Dahod offers quality technical education with a student-focused campus environment.

Explore Departments Official Website

About the College

Government Engineering College, Dahod is under the Commissionerate of Technical Education, Education Department, Government of Gujarat. It was established in June 2004 and the institute has a 17-acre campus.

The campus includes four academic blocks, a workshop block, a library block, and an amenities block with canteen, sports corner, and girls common room. Hostel facilities are available for both boys and girls.
